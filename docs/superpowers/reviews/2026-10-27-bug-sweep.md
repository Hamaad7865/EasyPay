## Confirmed

### 1. After Delete all transactions, every stock transfer of that account fails, for good

- `purge_transactions` (latest definition `db/migrations/0075_stock_counts.sql:329`) deletes `doc_counters` but its
  list of tables has no `stock_transfers` or `stock_transfer_lines`.
- `stock_transfers` has `unique (tenant_id, number)` (0138:63); `stock_transfer` takes `next_doc_number(p_tenant,
  'transfer')` (0138:158), which starts again at T-0001 once the counter is gone.
- So an account with two branches that made T-0001 to T-0003, then deleted its transactions on Backup and data:
  the next transfer is numbered T-0001, the insert breaks the unique key, the transaction rolls back (the counter
  with it), and every later try does the same. The page says "The transfer could not be made. Nothing was sent."
- Also: the purge deletes the transfers' movements and keeps the transfers and their printable notes.
- Clear test activity (`platform.clear_test_activity`, 0128) keeps the counters, so it does not have this.
- Fix (fix-forward from 0075's definition): add `'stock_transfer_lines','stock_transfers'` to the purge's list
  (`block_update` lets deletes through under `app.purge_tenant`). Add transfers to the purge test.
- **Fixed (`c4aee745`):** `0139_purge_transfers.sql`, as above. `db/tests/multi-branch` T12 (the restaurant: T-0001 and T-0002,
  the purge, then T-0001 and T-0002 again) failed with the duplicate key before it and passes after. On dev only.

### 2. Export, edit, import again at a second branch puts stock on it that is not there

- The export (`web/app/backoffice/items/export/route.ts:56,62`, unchanged in the range) writes each line's stock
  as the SUM over every branch, and its reorder point and quantity as the MAX over every branch.
- The import (`web/app/backoffice/items/import/actions.ts:37`) applies Opening stock and reorder levels at the
  branch picked, and `catalog_import` (0137, around line 284) skips opening stock only if the line moved, or holds
  stock, at THAT branch.
- So a shop with Main holding 10 Soap and Port Louis that never had Soap: the owner downloads the products to
  change prices (the import page's own lede suggests it), is refused with All branches (every line has Opening
  stock), picks Port Louis and imports. Port Louis gets +10 Soap that do not exist; `items.stock_qty` goes 10 to
  20. Every branch the file is imported at also gets the highest reorder levels of all branches.
- One branch: not affected.
- Fix: the export writes the picked branch's own stock and reorder levels (and leaves them empty with All
  branches), or the import ignores stock and reorder columns.
- **Fixed (`8daa6871`):** the first way. The download sits on the import page, beside the picker: with a branch
  picked the file has that branch's own stock and reorder levels (0 where it has none, never the account's total)
  and is named `products-<branch>.csv`; with All branches it has none, so it imports again with no branch. One
  branch: as before. `multi-branch` X1 to X6 go through the real route, the import screen's parser and the real
  action (X4 before: +10 Hand soap at Rose Hill, the total 10 to 20). Still possible: a file downloaded at one
  branch imported at another, and a file downloaded before the fix (opening stock is skipped only where the line
  moved or holds stock at that branch; a 0 in the file moves nothing).

### 3. A branch's phone (and address) saved before a till is on 0.9.29 never reaches that till

- The till's Room 35 to 36 (`android/.../core/database/Migrations.kt:483`) adds `stores.phone` without
  `UPDATE sync_state SET cursor = 0`, which the earlier migrations that add a pulled column do (lines 25, 37,
  59, ...). 34 to 35 added `stores.address` the same way.
- The server's `stores.phone` has existed since 0002. A till before build 41 pulls a store row and drops its
  phone; after updating, it never pulls that row again until it changes.
- So the owner saves each branch's phone on Company details (live now) while the tills are on 0.9.28; then the
  tills update. Each branch's receipts, Z report and emailed Z print the business's phone under the branch name
  (`PosSettings.kt:137` falls back) until someone saves that branch again.
- Workaround with no release: save Company details once more after every till of the account is on 0.9.29.
- Fix: the next Room migration resets the cursor (and a note to tell owners), or a server migration touches
  every store row once tills are on 41.
- **Fixed (`c8494cf1`):** Room 36 to 37 changes no table and pulls again from the start; the steps are one list,
  `Migrations.ALL`, which `AppModule` is given. `MigrationsTest` (a till on 35 or 36 pulls again after the phone
  column; the steps have no gap) failed before and passes; 903 unit tests pass. It reaches tablets with the next
  till build: until then the workaround above holds. Not seen on a tablet.

### 4. The emailed Z of a day that closed by itself is not the printed one

- `CashOps.dayDocOf` (`CashOps.kt:353`) rebuilds the closing without `carried`; for a close with no shift
  (`closeUnclosedAuto`) it also leaves `auto` false. The printed closing sets both (lines 612, 643).
- So a day that closed itself with 2 unpaid orders carried over: the paper says "Orders carried over 2" and
  "Closed by Automatically"; the email (and Print again) has neither.
- The TEST mark, the till's name, decimals and the branch header are read when the mail is sent, not at the
  close: a closing mailed after a rename shows the new name.
- Report content, not money.

### 5. The mail Worker's daily limits are one pool for every client

- `cloudflare/mail/src/rules.js:25,28` (`asks: 200`, `z: 1000`), counted in one Durable Object
  (`index.js:176,205`).
- About 50 confirmed addresses each sending 20 reports a day, or 20 addresses asking 10 confirmations, use the
  day up; then every client's Z waits ("the rest go tomorrow"), and a closing held back past 31 days
  (`ZMailRules.kt:54`) is skipped without a word. More than 1,000 tills sending would reach it with nobody
  misusing it.
- It fails closed: nobody gets mail they did not ask for.

### 6. A Z report too long for the Worker is never mailed

- `rules.js:88` refuses more than 400 lines (and over 64 KB is a 413); `ZMailRules.kt:135` takes any 4xx as
  final and moves past the closing. The only trace is a sentence in Settings. Unlikely with real days.

### 7. Receive without an order, All branches picked: costs pre-filled from the first branch

  average, whatever Arrived at says. A cost left as filled becomes the receiving branch's cost. A default only;

  Purchase orders and Counts list fewer. Cosmetic.

### 9. Delete all transactions fails for a shop that has issued a credit note (found while fixing 1, older)

- `credit_notes.refund_id` references `receipts` with RESTRICT (`credit_notes_tenant_id_refund_id_fkey`, 0109);
  `purge_transactions` deletes receipts and not credit notes. Run on a two-branch shop with one credit note, the
  purge stops with "update or delete on table "receipts" violates RESTRICT setting of foreign key constraint
  "credit_notes_tenant_id_refund_id_fkey" on table "credit_notes"", everything is rolled back, and Backup and data
  says "That could not be saved. Nothing was changed." (`web/lib/action.ts`). Of every key into the purge's tables from outside its list, this is the only one that
  blocks (read on dev, `pg_constraint`).
- A shop's returns give an exchange by default, which issues a credit note, so a shop that took one return while
  trying cannot delete its transactions. Live since credit notes (till 0.9.12).
- The purge also leaves deposits, account bills, loyalty points and MRA records; they do not block it.
- Not fixed: whether Delete all transactions removes credit notes (money owed to customers), with the others or
  not, or refuses in words, is the owner's to say.
- **Fixed (`725779a`), at the owner's "also deletes credit notes, deposits and account bills, as the till's Go live does":**
  `0140_purge_shop_money.sql` adds account payment lines, account payments, account bills, credit notes, deposit
  payments, deposit lines and deposits to the purge, in Clear test activity's order, and sets every customer's
  points to nothing (Go live and Clear test activity do both). MRA records stay. `db/tests/accounts` T12 (a sale
  earning points, an exchange kept on a note, a deposit, the bills of T1 to T9, then the purge as the owner) failed
  with the RESTRICT error above before it and passes after; Backup and data names them (`shop-pages` W1, R10). On
  dev only; production takes it with the next tag. What an open deposit took off the shelf stays off, as a sale's
  does.
