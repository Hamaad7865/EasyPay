### 1. Accepting a QR order in Look only tells the guest "accepted" and puts nothing on the table

**Path:**
- `till/core/guest/GuestBook.kt:121` writes the order as accepted before anything else happens.
- `:127-129` opens the table and adds the items. `TicketRepository.addItem` makes `ticket.create` and
  `ticket.add_line`, and its `op()` asks `SellCheck` (`till/core/data/TicketRepository.kt:91-93`).
- That throws `NotSelling` in each of three cases: someone came in to look only, the day is not open, or the
  day is closing itself.
- The `catch` at `:130-133` calls `kick.now()`. `GuestLink` (`till/core/guest/GuestLink.kt:159`) then sends the
  "accepted" answer, so the guest's phone says accepted.
- Neither entry point asks the gate first: the floor list (`till/feature/floor/Floor.kt:146-155`, `:358`) and
  the guest sheet (`till/feature/main/MainShell.kt:804`).

**Failing scenario:** a restaurant with PINs and QR ordering. Before the manager opens the day, or after the day
closed itself at night:
1. A guest orders from the table.
2. Someone in Look only taps Accept.
3. The guest is told the order was accepted.
4. The order leaves the waiting list. Nothing is on the table, and nothing reaches the kitchen.
5. The till says "Not every item of this order went on its table ... (You came in to look. Clock in to take
   orders.)" There is no list left to re-key the order from.

**Fix:** in `GuestBook.accept`, ask `SellCheck.check("ticket.add_line")` before `db.guest().answer(...)`. The
cashier then gets the gate's words, and the order stays waiting.

### 2. An MRA credit note whose sale is "to check" holds back every later invoice of the till

**Path:**
- `till/core/mra/MraQueue.kt:28-31` stops the batch at a CRN whose sale on this till is neither fiscalised nor
  refused.
- A sale sent twice with no answer becomes `check` (`till/core/database/Mra.kt:72`). It is never sent again
  until a manager presses Send again on it (`MraPage.kt:212`).
- So once such a sale is refunded, every later sale and refund of that till waits behind the credit note.
- The stop is meant: `MraQueueTest.kt:26` asserts it, and the plan says it. What is not handled is the result.
  `till/core/mra/MraBanner.kt` never reads `counts.check`. After an hour it says "N invoices are waiting for the
  MRA since HH:MM. They go once the line is back." They do not go: they wait for the manager.

**How a sale reaches `check`:** two sends, each with no answer.
- The first is at payment, with about 10 s to answer (`MraSender.PAY_MS`).
- The second comes 2 minutes later, from the worker or from the next sale's send, with 30 s.
- A slow or dropping line is enough. So is a gateway's HTML 5xx page, which `MraQueue.read` counts as "no
  answer" (see P1).

**Fix (the owner's pick):**
- (a) let a CRN whose sale is `check` go anyway: the MRA answers for each invoice on its own. The cost: that
  CRN may come back refused, and a refused one is never sent again, even after Send again fiscalises its sale; or
- (b) keep the stop, and have the banner name the sale to check as the reason the rest wait. The back office
  page already lists "sent twice with no answer".

### 3. An exchange of another till's sale costs the customer the points the goods earned

**Path:**
- `till/core/data/Exchange.kt:102-104` puts the customer on the new sale from `db.tickets().ticket(orig.ticket_id)`.
- Tills do not pull tickets: `till/core/sync/PullApplier.kt` has no `tickets`. For a receipt pulled from
  another till, that ticket is not on the tablet, so the new sale has no customer and earns nothing.
- The refund half still takes back what the goods earned. It reads `orig.customer_id` and `orig.points_earned`,
  which the pull did bring (`till/core/data/OrderOps.kt:506-507`; `PullApplier.kt:426-429`).
- The same happens on one till after a reinstall, since its receipts then come from the pull.

**Failing scenario:**
1. At till 1, a customer earns Rs 10 on a Rs 1,000 sale (1%).
2. They exchange it like for like at till 2.
3. Till 2 takes back Rs 10, and the new sale earns nothing. The customer is Rs 10 down.

This is the case `8aae4603` fixed for the same till.

**Fix:** take `orig.customer_id` first, then the ticket's.

### 4. The customer display never shows points used on Pay

**Path:**
- `till/core/display/DisplayFeed.kt:96-106` works the sale out from the order and `tickets.pendingDiscount()`.
- The points chosen on Pay are held only in the Pay screen's `points` (`till/feature/pay/Pay.kt`), so the display
  keeps the total and Balance from before the points.
- After payment, `Pictures.paid` (`till/core/display/Pictures.kt:74`) keeps the sale's lines only when
  `sale.total == total`. With points used, the two totals differ, so the paid screen drops the lines and shows
  the total alone.

**Fix:** hand the points pick to the feed as `pendingDiscount` is handed, and count it in the picture.

### 5. `MIN_TILL_VERSION` was never raised, and loyalty has no build check

**Evidence:**
- `db/migrations/0130_customer_displays.sql:40-41` says the release that carries it raises `MIN_TILL_VERSION`.
- The repo's variables (`gh variable list`) hold only `TILL_AUTH_URL` and `TILL_FUNCTION_URL`. The deploy jobs'
  `production` environment (`.github/workflows/deploy.yml:106`) holds none (`gh api .../environments/production/variables`:
  0). So production runs with `MIN_TILL_VERSION` = 0 (`neon.ts:9`), and every build is let in.
- The back office lets loyalty be switched on whatever the tills' builds. Auto-close has such a check
  (`AUTO_CLOSE_BUILD`, `web/app/backoffice/settings/page.tsx:242`); loyalty only says so in a sentence.

**What an older till then does:**
- Older than build 33, with loyalty on: it sends no points. A refund made on it never takes back what the sale
  earned, so the customer keeps points for goods returned.
- It lists a customer display as a printer that does not answer.
- It prints "57 in 80 mm" as 58 mm.

**Fix:**
- The owner's: set `MIN_TILL_VERSION` (33 at least).
- Ours: an old-tills line on the Loyalty card, built as auto-close's is.

### 6. `delete-client.cjs` cannot remove a client that ever sent an MRA training invoice

**Path:**
- `db/migrations/0132_mra_till.sql:119-120` guards `mra_records` with `block_update`.
- `block_update` lets a delete through only when `app.purge_tenant` names the client
  (`0051_purge_transactions.sql:24-28`).
- `db/scripts/delete-client.cjs:45` turns off only `trg_no_update`, `trg_draft_only` and `trg_open_only`, and
  sets no `app.purge_tenant`. Its delete loop catches only foreign-key and restrict errors (`:107`).

**Checked on dev, in one transaction that was rolled back:** as `neondb_owner`,
`delete from mra_records where tenant_id = ...` gives `P0001 table mra_records is insert-only`.

**Result:** the script stops with that error for any client that has records. It refuses in plain words only
the clients that went live (`:62`). 0132's own comment says the records go "by the owner's purge of a whole
client".

**Fix:** either set `app.purge_tenant` for the block in delete-client, or refuse every client with
`mra_records` in words.

### 7. `unit-has-stock` reaches the till as "error"

**Path:**
- `db/migrations/0131_sold_by.sql:107` raises `unit-has-stock` from `push_item_save`.
- `sync_push`'s list of codes leaves it out: the rebuilt one in `0132_mra_till.sql:291`, and dev's live
  definition (dumped and searched). So `0132_mra_till.sql:524-528` turns it into `error`.
- The till's own words for it (`till/core/data/ItemForm.kt:159`) are never shown. It says "The server refused it
  (error)."
- `db/tests/sold-by.test.cjs:35-38` calls `push_item_save` directly, so the suite passes.

**Fix:** a migration that adds `unit-has-stock` to `sync_push`'s codes.

## Plausible, not proven

- **P1. An MRA outage answered with HTML 5xx pages leaves its invoices to a manager.**
  - `MraQueue.read` (`till/core/mra/MraQueue.kt:57`) counts a non-JSON 5xx as "no answer". That is cautious:
    such a request may have been fiscalised. The MRA's own 500 (ERR0023) is JSON and is retried.
  - Two such sends put every invoice of the batch in `check`, within a couple of sales.
  - After the outage, each one needs its own Send again. The till's list shows 50 at most (`Mra.kt` `problems()`).
  - Whether a gateway page means "not reached" is the MRA's to know. Worth deciding with finding 2.
- **P2. A refund of a sale made before the client went live with the MRA.**
  - It becomes a CRN that names the receipt number (`MraIssuer.kt:59`), which the MRA never saw as an invoice.
  - Whether the MRA refuses that is unknown: the sandbox has only seen CRNs of fiscalised sales.
  - This will be common in a new MRA client's first weeks.
- **P3. An EBS ID moved to a till that had another EBS.**
  - `set_till_mra_ids` (`0132_mra_till.sql:672-673`) and `MraChain.merge` keep, for each document type, the
    higher counter of the two.
  - So the till goes on from its old EBS's counter, with a hash of the old EBS's last invoice.
  - A fresh till row is fine. The sandbox does not check the chain.
- **P4. A credit note of a sale pulled from another till.**
  - Its lines come from the receipt (`MraIssuer.lineOf`): no add-on names, no SKU, and the business's default
    goods or services, not the category's.
  - So its lines read differently from the STD it corrects.
- **P5. Loyalty: a stale balance on a till with a sale still to send.**
  - While that sale waits, the pull keeps the till's own balance (`PullApplier.kt:288-296`).
  - If that sale earned exactly what it used, the server never changes the customer's row again. The balance
    another till moved in the meantime then stays out of this till until the customer changes again.
- **P6. The display's "Total quantity" for kilo lines.** `Pictures.kt:64` rounds each line to whole units, so
  0.25 kg counts as 0 and 0.5 kg as 1. Cosmetic.

## Checked and found sound

- **Functions redefined by two migrations kept each other's edits:**
  - `push_printer_save`: 0127's 57 is in 0130's.
  - `push_settings_save`: 0128's loyalty is in 0133's.
  - `push_receipt_create` (0128) keeps 0124's service-charge limit of 3000.
- **Room:** 31 to 35 are each registered (`AppModule.kt:35`), with no destructive fallback.
- **MRA invoice figures:**
  - The amounts come to the receipt's total before cash rounding. That holds with the discount spread as Calc
    spreads it, VAT added or included, the service charge as its own line, and points as a discount before VAT.
    The server flags any difference (`differs`).
  - The chain is kept per document type, as the MRA's data structure says ("last invoice for each type").
  - An exchange sends its CRN before its STD, in one request.
- **Loyalty sums:**
  - Refund shares of points settle exactly: the last refund takes what is left.
  - The server keeps the till's figures within bounds, so Kotlin and Postgres rounding never meet.
- **Auto-close:**
  - Traced cases: quiet before the cut-off, a busy bar past it, a day opened after the set time.
  - The two automatic ops carry no employee, and 0133 takes them as the till's login.
  - The Z prints NOT COUNTED.
  - Every op helper that moves money asks the gate, apart from finding 1.
- **Settings saves:** they merge into the settings (`saveSettings`, `push_settings_save`), so no form resets
  another form's keys. The customer card is kept on every back office save.
- **Displays:** they stay out of every paper list (`Routing.silent`). The server refuses turning a display into
  a printer, or a printer into a display.
- **The import's new columns:** Sold by and Count stock are read only from their own headers.
- **The MRA page:**
  - Its day filters use each branch's time zone.
  - Credit notes show with a minus.
  - CSV cells with a comma, a semicolon, a quote or a line break are quoted.
- **`hello.ts` gates:** they compare build numbers as numbers.
