# Bug sweep, 2026-11-03: offline clients and Premium

Asked for by the owner before working on EasyPay alone: "make sure we're all set to go with offline clients
and premium ... do a complete check and finalise everything", MRA left out.

How it was checked:
- every dev-safe database suite, one at a time, on `dev-review`;
- the tablet's unit tests;
- three read-only reviews of the code:
  - the offline tablet (`core/data/LocalAnswers`, `core/sync`, the Room side);
  - plans and Premium (`set_tenant_plan`, /admin, the till API, sync);
  - EasyPay in Claude (`web/lib/assistant`).

Each finding below was read again in the code before it was fixed. A fix was written with a test that fails without it, wherever a test could reach it.

## Not fixed: what the owner does instead

These are in the Owner's Manual ("Running it on your own").

- **A client still "Being set up" on the server** keeps Clear test activity and Remove armed. The tablet's own Go live doesn't tell the server, and 0153 covers only plan changes made after it. **Press Go live in /admin for every client at handover, Offline ones too.** Clients made Premium before 0153 need it pressed by hand.
- **An Offline client's company details stay the tablet's.** The first-run Company step queues a `company.save`, and `OwnSettings` keeps `company` while that change waits. On an Offline tablet it waits for good. **Change the name, address, VAT number and BRN on the tablet** (This till → Set-up → Business details), not through Login to back office.
- **A replaced tablet that is still signed in can take its till back.** "Replace tablet" ends its key. But a tablet that is signed in with a login gets a new key at its next pull, and that locks the replacement out. **Switch the old tablet off, or clear its data, the moment it is replaced.**
- **At Connect, what a tablet kept is written over newer edits made in the back office.** That covers prices, categories, taxes, roles and staff. **While a client is offline, change its menu in one place only: on the tablet, or through Login to back office followed by Get changes before anyone edits on the tablet.**
- **A replacement tablet set up without the old one's backup starts its receipt numbers again** (an offline till never told the server its numbers). **Always restore the backup on the new tablet.**
- **Stock entered in an offline client's back office never reaches its tablet.** Get changes keeps the tablet's own quantities. **Count stock on the tablet.**
- **Pictures chosen on an offline tablet stay on that tablet**, even after it goes Premium.
- **An offline tablet has no screen for the return policy, the return window, credit note months or the slow-mover thresholds.** EasyPay sets them through Login to back office, and the tablet takes them with Get changes.
- **On an offline tablet, sub categories don't follow their parent's printers or colour.** Give each sub its own.
- **Codes made by tablets before till 0.9.36 can still collide** (finding 8 covers new codes only). If a product is refused `barcode-taken` at Connect, give it a new barcode in the back office and tap Connect again.
- **Anyone can wake the database** through `/devices/redeem` and the till key check, without a real code or key. This finding is plausible only. A rate-limit rule in Cloudflare (Security → WAF → Rate limiting) on `api.easypaypos.com/devices/*` closes it.
- **Pictures are never deleted, and a business has no cap on them** (0143), so the database grows with every picture replaced. Plausible.
- **A file re-imported without the Sub category column** (made before `6c073662`, or by hand) puts products in a new top category named after the sub. Export again before you import.
- **Smaller differences on an offline tablet:** a new stock line starts at average cost 0 (the server uses the line's cost), and when no tax is marked default the tablet picks a different one.
