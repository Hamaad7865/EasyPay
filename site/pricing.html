Does EasyPay do MRA e-invoicing?

Yes, with the Premium MRA plan, for businesses that must e-invoice. Your legal name, TAN, BRN and VAT number are set once in the back office, each category is marked as goods or services, and each tax carries its MRA code. Your BRN and VAT number print on every receipt, an item can be at 15%, zero-rated or exempt, and the back office has a tax report laid out for your VAT return. Does it work when the internet goes down?

Yes. OrdersSales and payments are saved on the tablet and sent to the back office when the connection returns. Printing goes over your own network or a USB cable and does not need the internet at all. Opening and closing the day carry on too. What waits for the connection: changes to the menuyour products, prices and staff, and setting up a new tablet.

Which printers does it work with?

ESC/POS receipt printers with 58 mm or 80 mm paper, connected to your network by their IP address or to the tablet by USB cable. Bluetooth printers are not supported yet.

Can I take card payments?

Yes, alongside your card terminal. Take the payment on the terminal, then record it as a card payment on the till. The till does not talk to the terminal itself.

Can you build a feature we need?

Ask us. Customers can request custom features, and we look at building them around the way your business works. Tell us what you need when you book a demo, or on WhatsApp.

Can I bring in the products I already have?

Yes. The back office reads a spreadsheet of your products, with their sizes, colours, prices and barcodes, shows you what it understood and adds them in one go. After that, products and prices are yours to change there, and tills pick the changes up at their next sync.

Do I need a barcode scanner?

No, but it is faster with one. Every product has a tile to tap and can be found by its name. A scanner that types like a keyboard, by USB or Bluetooth, works as it is. Scanning with the tablet's camera is not supported yet.

My products have no barcodes. Can it make them?

Yes. The back office gives a product a barcode of its own and prints labels for it, on a sheet or on a roll, for you to stick on.

Can I change the menu and prices myself?

Yes. Categories, items, add-ons, taxes and prices are all yours to edit in the back office. Tablets pick the changes up at their next sync.

Which languages does the till speak?

English, French and Kreol Morisien. The language is chosen on the tablet, from the side menu, and can be changed at any time.
