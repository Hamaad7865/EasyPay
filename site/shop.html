How much does a POS system for a shop cost?

EasyPay's Starter pack is Rs 5,000, paid once for each tablet. It comes with the till, the back office, your stock and your reports. The tablet, the printer and the scanner are yours to choose.

Does it keep selling when the internet goes down?

Yes. Sales and payments are kept on the tablet and sent to the back office when the connection returns, and receipts print over your own network or a USB cable. Changes to products, prices and staff wait for the connection, and so does setting up a new tablet.

Does EasyPay do MRA e-invoicing?

Yes, with the Premium MRA plan, for businesses that must e-invoice. Your legal name, TAN, BRN and VAT number are set once in the back office, each product category is marked as goods or services, and each tax carries its MRA code. Tell us when you book a demo and we will set it up with you.

I have more than one shop. Does it handle branches?

Yes. One account holds all your branches. Each branch has its own tills, printers and stock, and the catalog, the staff and the prices are shared between them.

Can I bring in the products I already have?

Yes. The back office reads a spreadsheet of your products with their sizes, colours, prices and barcodes, shows you what it understood, and adds them in one go.

Do I need a barcode scanner?

No, but a counter is faster with one. Any scanner that types like a keyboard works, by USB or Bluetooth, and every product also has a tile to tap. Scanning with the tablet's camera is not supported yet.

My products have no barcodes. What then?

The back office gives a product a barcode of its own and prints its labels, on a sheet or on a roll, for you to stick on.

Can I take card payments?

Yes, on your own card terminal. Take the payment there, then record it on the till as a card payment. The till does not talk to the terminal itself.

Can you add a feature my shop needs?

Ask us. Customers can request custom features, and we look at building them around the way your shop works. Tell us what you need when you book a demo, or on WhatsApp.
