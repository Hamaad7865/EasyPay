Send once. The right printer gets the right ticket.

Payment

Split it any way the table asks. It still adds up to one bill.

  • Equally, by seat or line by line

    Divide the amount into equal shares, each paid its own way, or move lines between separate checks. The shares are kept until they add up to the bill.

  • Cash with the change worked out

    A keypad, round amounts and the change to give. The service charge you set in the back office sits on the bill as its own line.

  • A receipt the guest can keep

    Print it on 58 mm or 80 mm paper with your logo and your own header and footer, or share it from the tablet by e-mail or WhatsApp.

  • Refunds without the guesswork

    Refund a whole receipt or only some of its lines, with a reason and how the money goes back. Reprint any receipt exactly as it was first printed.

Selling

Scan it, tap it, sell it. The sale builds itself.

  • A barcode or a tile

    A scanner that types like a keyboard rings a product up by its barcode, and every product has a tile to tap. The same product again adds one more to its line.

  • Sizes, colours and weight

    A product that comes in sizes or colours asks which one, and each choice says what is left. One sold by weight asks for the kilos.

  • A discount, or another price

    Ten or twenty percent off a line in one tap, your own figure, or a discount on the whole sale. A changed price holds for this sale only, and can ask for a manager.

  • Serve the next customer first

    Park the sale as it is and start another. Parked sales wait behind one key until their customer is back at the counter.

Returns

Scan the receipt. The sale opens, ready to put right.

  • The receipt finds itself

    Every receipt prints a barcode at its foot. Scan it and the sale opens. Without the paper, find it by its number: the last 30 days are there, from every till in the shop.

  • Refund what comes back

    Take off what the customer keeps, pick how the money goes back and give a reason. The goods go back into stock, or are written off as damaged when they are faulty.

  • An exchange in one sale

    What comes back pays for what the customer takes instead, and only the difference is paid or given back.

  • Paid the wrong way?

    Change how a receipt was paid without touching its amount. Who corrected it, and when, is kept.

Questions

What restaurants ask us first.

What shops ask us first.

Does it work when the internet goes down?

Yes. OrdersSales and payments are saved on the tablet and sent to the back office when the connection returns. Printing goes over your own network or a USB cable and does not need the internet at all.

Which printers does it work with?

ESC/POS receipt printers with 58 mm or 80 mm paper, connected to your network by their IP address or to the tablet by USB cable. Bluetooth printers are not supported yet.

Can I take card payments?

Yes, alongside your card terminal. Take the payment on the terminal, then record it as a card payment on the till. The till does not talk to the terminal itself.

Can I bring in the products I already have?

Yes. The back office reads a spreadsheet of your products, with their sizes, colours, prices and barcodes, shows you what it understood and adds them in one go. After that, products and prices are yours to change there, and tills pick the changes up at their next sync.

Do I need a barcode scanner?

No, but it is faster with one. Every product has a tile to tap and can be found by its name. A scanner that types like a keyboard, by USB or Bluetooth, works as it is. Scanning with the tablet's camera is not supported yet.

My products have no barcodes. Can it make them?

Yes. The back office gives a product a barcode of its own and prints labels for it, on a sheet or on a roll, for you to stick on.

Can I change the menu and prices myself?

Yes. Categories, items, add-ons, taxes and prices are all yours to edit in the back office. Tablets pick the changes up at their next sync.

Which languages does the till speak?

English, French and Kreol Morisien. The language is chosen on the tablet, from the side menu, and can be changed at any time.

How do I get an account?

We open every account ourselves. Book a demo, and once you are happy we create your restaurant and your logins. From there the menu, the tables and the staff are yours to set up in the back office.
